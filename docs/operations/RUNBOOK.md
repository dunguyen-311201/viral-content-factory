# Operator Runbook

## Daily workflow
1. Capture topic signals from permitted sources.
2. Check permission and verification status.
3. Group related signals into trends.
4. Review trend score and evidence.
5. Generate a brief and content package.
6. Read every line; verify factual claims.
7. Run quality review.
8. Resolve findings and obtain human approval.
9. Render and preview media.
10. Confirm final media, export, and publish manually.
11. Record post URL and metrics later.

## Before publishing checklist
- [ ] Source usage is permitted or content is genuinely original.
- [ ] No private individual is identifiable without a valid basis.
- [ ] Facts and allegations are clearly distinguished.
- [ ] No unsupported statistic, quote, or causal claim.
- [ ] Script is original and not a close paraphrase.
- [ ] Visuals, fonts, music, and voice assets are owned/licensed.
- [ ] AI review findings are resolved.
- [ ] Human approval exists for this exact content version.
- [ ] Final rendered media was previewed and confirmed.
- [ ] CTA does not invite harassment or dogpiling.

## If content is challenged
- Pause further distribution where possible.
- Record the complaint and affected publication.
- Review source, script version, approval, and media.
- Correct, remove, or clarify where warranted.
- Record the decision and prevention action.
