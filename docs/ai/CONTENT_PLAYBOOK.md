# Content Playbook — Góc Khuất (Hypothesis v1)

## Brand promise
“Những câu chuyện khiến bạn nhìn cuộc sống theo một cách khác.”

## Voice
- Vietnamese natural spoken style.
- Curious, empathetic, grounded, not preachy.
- Clear and concise; avoid fake expertise.
- Strong opening, but no fabricated cliffhangers or manipulative claims.
- Distinguish “theo góc nhìn”, “có người cho rằng”, and verified facts.

## Content pillars
1. Relationships (35% test allocation): boundaries, communication, expectations, emotional labor.
2. Workplace (25%): teamwork, managers, credit, burnout, professional boundaries.
3. Social psychology (25%): habits, cognitive bias, group behavior, everyday choices.
4. Social discussion (15%): public conversations with adequate sourcing and low privacy risk.

## Short-video structure (45–90 seconds)
1. Hook (0–3s): question, contrast, or surprising but supportable observation.
2. Context (3–15s): explain the situation without naming private people.
3. Tension (15–45s): show competing perspectives.
4. Insight (45–70s): explain what may be happening; label interpretation as interpretation.
5. CTA (last 5–10s): invite thoughtful discussion, not harassment or dogpiling.

## Hook patterns
- “Có một kiểu mâu thuẫn ở công sở mà nhiều người từng gặp…”
- “Đôi khi vấn đề không nằm ở việc ai đúng, mà là…”
- “Vì sao một câu nói tưởng bình thường lại khiến người khác khó chịu?”
Use as patterns, not repetitive templates.

## Editorial checklist
- Is the topic useful or meaningfully interesting?
- Is the source permitted and traceable?
- Are facts separated from allegations and opinions?
- Could a viewer identify a private person?
- Does the script copy distinctive wording or narrative structure?
- Is the hook accurate?
- Does the CTA invite discussion without targeting anyone?
- Would we publish this if the subject were someone we know?

## Carousel structure
1. Cover: one clear question or tension.
2. Context: explain the situation.
3. Perspective A.
4. Perspective B.
5. Analysis or practical insight.
6. Takeaway and discussion prompt.

## Avoid
- “100% sự thật”, “ai cũng biết”, “chắc chắn là…”
- Unverified accusations and private screenshots.
- Naming/shaming private individuals.
- Medical, legal, or financial advice presented as professional advice.
- Rage bait, fabricated quotes, fake statistics, and engagement manipulation.
