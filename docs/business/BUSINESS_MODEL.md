# Business Model and Validation Plan

## Initial model
Owned channels first. The product is an internal production system, not a SaaS offering.

## Audience-building sequence
1. Validate a consistent editorial identity.
2. Publish consistently with quality and source discipline.
3. Identify topics/formats that attract meaningful watch time, saves, shares, and follows.
4. Test affiliate offers only when genuinely relevant and disclosed.
5. Consider sponsorship after audience trust and predictable reach exist.
6. Consider digital products/community only after repeated audience demand is observed.

## Revenue cautions
- No guaranteed revenue or virality.
- Platform monetization rules and eligibility vary and change.
- Do not buy followers, views, or engagement.
- Disclose sponsorships and affiliate relationships as required.
- Do not allow commercial incentives to override editorial safety.

## Budget envelope (planning estimate)
Target: under VND 3,000,000/month during validation.
- LLM usage: ~VND 700,000
- TTS: ~VND 350,000
- VPS/hosting: ~VND 300,000
- Storage: ~VND 100,000
- Licensed assets/tools: ~VND 250,000
- Reserve: ~VND 800,000

These are placeholders for planning. Confirm actual provider pricing, taxes, usage limits, and exchange rates before committing. Start with mock providers and local rendering.
